The other day I was driving to the Rammstein concert in my Porsche.
I got hungry on the way and had to stop by the Baeckerei at the train station, since it was the only place I could get a warm Broetchen on a Sunday.
As I walked into the Baeckerei, I saw an older gentleman on his Drahtesel ,followed by his Hund - a dapper little dachsund.
I asked the gentleman what his Hund's name was and he replied Sauerkraut.
Before I could introduce myself, Sauerkraut snatched an Apfelkuchen from the bakery counter and sprinted toward the Biergarten like he owned the place.
The gentleman gave a hearty chuckle, which sounded like a Staubsauger, and explained that Sauerkraut is known as the Mehlhase inspector of this Baeckerei.
Before I could reply, I looked out the window and realized that my dear Porsche was being towed off the Hauptstrasse!
I ran back to try and save my Porsche but a lady in a Dirndel stopped me at the door and said the only way I could get it back would be if I raced the tow truck on the Drahtesel, they were very wettbewerbsfaehig in this town.
To my surprise, the lady in the Dirndel was actually my professor, Dr. Mehlhase, who pulled on her racing Handschuhe and offered to help me race the tow truck on the Drahtesel.
Feeling hundemuede after the long day, I decided to switch on the Klimaanlage to cool down.
To lift my spirits, I put on a track that instantly turned into a solid Ohrwurm while my inner Pantoffelheld took over and refused to leave the couch.
Just as I was getting comfortable, Sauerkraut the Hund burst through my front door wearing a pair of Handschuhe and demanded that I help him recover my stolen Porsche.
